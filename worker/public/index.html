37Studio.

Video and social media studio that runs on your own computer.

Download

  1. Install Docker Desktop and start it.
  2. Unzip 37studio.zip anywhere, then double-click Start 37Studio (start.bat on Windows, start.command on Mac). It downloads the app the first time and updates itself on every start.
  3. In the app, open Settings: paste your license key and your Claude key, then add your company.
