Under the hood

An AI agent that edits like a designer

No templates. Each video is written from scratch as an animated web page, then rendered to MP4 frame by frame on your computer.

  1. 1
    Reads the companyThe knowledge base, design system and brand library come into every brief.
  2. 2
    Gathers materialCaptures the pages it needs, pulls the company's own clips and transcribes the audio with timings.
  3. 3
    Writes the compositionLayout, motion, captions and timing on a HyperFrames timeline. Footage is cut with ffmpeg; Gemini and ElevenLabs are called for new images, clips, voice or music.
  4. 4
    Checks its own workRenders stills and looks at them like a reviewer: text, safe zones, brand colours. Fixes what's off. You watch the live preview, ask for changes or move things on the timeline.
  5. 5
    Renders and hands overHeadless Chrome draws every frame and ffmpeg encodes the MP4, on your PC. Claude writes a caption for each platform.

Four steps to your first video

37Studio runs inside Docker Desktop, which keeps it and its tools in one sealed box on your computer.

Windows 10 and 11macOS, Apple Silicon (M1–M4)macOS, IntelLinux

16 GB RAM recommended, and about 10 GB free disk for the app and your footage.

  1. Install Docker Desktop. It's free for personal use and small businesses.
  2. Download 37Studio and unzip it anywhere, for example in Documents.
  3. Double-click start.bat on Windows or start.command on a Mac. The first start downloads about 3 GB; every later start checks for updates.
  4. 37Studio opens in its own window. In Settings, add your Claude key, then your first company. Make two videos free; when you're ready, buy a license and paste the key in Settings.

FAQ

Questions people ask

Is 37Studio free?

The download is free and you can make two videos to try it. After that, a license is ৳5,000 for six months or ৳8,000 for a year (about $40 or $65): message us on WhatsApp or email jahangir@shaitrish.com. AI usage is separate and billed to your own Claude plan or Anthropic, Google and ElevenLabs accounts.

Can I control how the AI replies?

Yes, per company. Choose off, drafts for approval or replies on their own; write rules for tone, sign-off, what never to promise and when to hand over to a person; and keep the knowledge base it answers from up to date. Facts only ever come from that knowledge base.

Do I need a server?

No. Everything runs on your own Windows PC or Mac inside Docker Desktop. The only things that leave your computer are calls to the AI providers and the social platforms you connect.

What happens when my computer is off?

Nothing is lost. Messenger and Instagram keep messages on Meta, and WhatsApp messages wait for you for 30 days. When 37Studio starts again it answers everything the platform still allows a reply to: messages from the last 24 hours and comments up to two days old. For round-the-clock replies, keep it on a computer that doesn't sleep, such as a Mac mini.

Can I use my Claude subscription instead of an API key?

Yes. Run claude setup-token in Claude Code on your computer and paste the token in Settings → Keys; the agent then works within your Pro or Max plan. Anthropic's terms govern using a subscription in other apps, so an Anthropic API key is the fully supported route.

Which AI does it use?

Claude Opus 5.5 for editing by default, with Sonnet 5.5 and Haiku 5.5 for quick changes and DeepSeek through OpenCode for cheaper routine edits. Gemini makes images and video clips, and ElevenLabs makes voiceovers, sound effects and music. Models, keys and prices are set in Settings, and new tools arrive with updates.

Does it only make videos?

No. It also makes thumbnails, square and portrait posts, announcements and covers in each company's brand, and can generate images with Gemini. Videos post straight to Facebook and Instagram; images download as PNG for now.

Which platforms can it post to?

Facebook Pages and Instagram business accounts today, many per company. YouTube and TikTok publishing is built and coming soon. Facebook Groups and personal profiles can't be posted to by any app: Meta removed that in 2024 and 2018.

Does WhatsApp work?

Yes, in beta, for a number on the WhatsApp Business API. Keeping the same number on the WhatsApp Business phone app at the same time needs Meta to approve our app as a Tech Provider first, so for now use a number dedicated to the API.

Why is the first download so big?

About 3 GB, once. 37Studio bundles every tool its AI agent works with, so you install nothing else: Claude Code and OpenCode, the HyperFrames video engine with its own Chrome for rendering, ffmpeg for cutting and encoding, Node.js, Python, and fonts for Bangla, English and emoji. Updates after that only download what changed.

Will it keep getting better?

Yes. We use 37Studio for our own products and keep improving it. Every update released while your license runs is included and installs itself the next time you start the app. YouTube and TikTok publishing, more AI tools and image posts are next on our list.

Does it work in Bangla?

Yes. Videos, voiceovers, captions and replies can all be in Bangla, and replies follow the customer's language and script. Long Bangla recordings, like a 2-hour class, are transcribed on your own computer with an open Whisper model: no per-minute fees, and the audio never leaves your PC.

Where is my data? Do you collect anything?

Everything lives in a data folder next to the app on your computer: companies, footage, finished videos, messages and settings, with your keys encrypted. We don't collect it, and the license is checked offline. The privacy page lists the few things that pass through our server, such as the update check.
