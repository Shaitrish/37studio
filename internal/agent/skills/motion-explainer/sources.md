# Free-licence sources for motion explainers

Check the licence of every single file, not the site. Record each one in work/credits.md (what, author, licence, link), put a short credit on screen while it shows, and the full list in the post text.

Safe for a brand: public domain, CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA (credit, and say it's CC BY-SA), the Pexels and Pixabay licences, and government works that are public domain. Not safe: NonCommercial (NC), NoDerivatives (ND), "editorial use only", anything with no licence stated, and copyrighted film or TV clips, even short ones.

## Pictures

| Need | Where | Licence notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Archive photos, portraits, documents, engravings, old maps | Wikimedia Commons (commons.wikimedia.org) | Per file: read the licence box; old works are usually public domain |
| Paintings, prints, objects | The Met Open Access, Rijksmuseum, Art Institute of Chicago, Smithsonian Open Access, Cleveland Museum of Art | CC0 when marked open access |
| Old books, maps, newspapers | Library of Congress, British Library (Flickr Commons), Internet Archive book scans | Mostly public domain; check each item |
| Earth from space, planets | NASA (Visible Earth, Earth Observatory, Blue Marble, images.nasa.gov) | Public domain; not the NASA logo; credit "NASA" |
| Satellite close-ups of a place | Copernicus Sentinel-2 (Copernicus Browser), USGS Landsat (EarthExplorer) | Free; credit "Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data [year]" or "USGS" |
| Planet and globe textures | Solar System Scope textures | CC BY 4.0 |

## Maps and geodata

| Need | Where | Licence notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Countries, coastlines, rivers, lakes, populated places (vector) | Natural Earth (naturalearthdata.com), 1:110m for world shots, 1:10m for one country | Public domain |
| Roads, rivers, borders, buildings at street level, historical OSM states | OpenStreetMap (Overpass API, Geofabrik extracts) | ODbL: credit "© OpenStreetMap contributors" |
| Terrain for a painted relief | SRTM or Copernicus DEM (OpenTopography, USGS) | Free with credit |
| Earthquakes, faults, volcanoes | USGS earthquake catalogue (earthquake.usgs.gov) | Public domain |
| Historical boundaries | Wikimedia Commons scans of old maps; the gazette or treaty page itself | Check each scan |

Keep map data small: simplify to the zoom you show and keep the points that straight borders need. Unwrap rings that cross 180°.

## Data

World Bank Open Data (CC BY 4.0), Our World in Data (CC BY), UN data portals, national statistics offices and official gazettes. Quote the exact figure and year, and say "about" when sources differ.

## Footage and sound

| Need | Where | Licence notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Moving footage | Pexels, Pixabay, Wikimedia Commons video, NASA video | Free; credit anyway |
| YouTube clips | Only videos marked Creative Commons (CC BY), with credit | Never ordinary uploads, news broadcasts or films |
| Sound effects | /media-use (Openverse CC0 / CC BY, the bundled Pixabay library) or `37studio gen sfx` | Credit CC BY sounds |
| Fonts | Google Fonts, Noto | SIL OFL |

## Facts

Check every claim in two independent sources (an encyclopaedia of the country, the original document, a university page) before writing the line. Write work/research.md with the claim, sources and the wording you chose. Say "scholars think" where they do only think so. Never present a guess as a measurement.
